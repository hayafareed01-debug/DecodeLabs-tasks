PHISHING AWARENESS ANALYSIS
Sample 3: Fake Social Media Security Alert
========================================

ORIGINAL MESSAGE
----------------------------------------

Security Alert

We noticed a new login attempt on your account.

If this was not you, confirm your identity immediately
to prevent your account from being locked.

Verify your account here:

https://account-security.example.com

You must complete verification within 12 hours.

Thank you,
Security Team


SUSPICIOUS KEYWORDS / PHRASES
----------------------------------------

• Security Alert
• new login attempt
• not you
• immediately
• account locked
• Verify your account
• within 12 hours


SUSPICIOUS LINK
----------------------------------------

https://account-security.example.com

Reason:
The domain does not identify the official social media
platform and is presented as an account verification link.


PHISHING RED FLAGS
----------------------------------------

1. The message creates fear about an unauthorized login.
2. It threatens that the account may be locked.
3. Urgent language is used.
4. The recipient is asked to verify their identity.
5. A suspicious link is provided.
6. The sender is only identified as "Security Team."


WHY THIS MESSAGE IS UNSAFE
----------------------------------------

This message is potentially unsafe because it creates fear
about account security and pressures the recipient to act
quickly. The recipient should verify the alert through the
official application or website instead of using the link
provided in the message.


RECOMMENDED ACTION
----------------------------------------

Do not click the link or provide login credentials.

Open the official social media application or type the
official website address manually to check for security
alerts. If necessary, change the password through the
official platform.
