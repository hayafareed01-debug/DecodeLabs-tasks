#  Phishing Awareness Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Cyber Security Internship at Decode Labs**.

The purpose of this project is to analyze common phishing messages, identify their warning signs, understand the risks associated with them, and provide practical recommendations for staying safe from phishing attacks.

The project focuses on three fictional phishing scenarios:

-  Fake Bank Account Alert
-  Fake Prize / Reward Message
-  Fake Social Media Security Alert

---

##  Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Understand the concept of phishing and social engineering.
- Identify suspicious keywords and phrases.
- Analyze suspicious links and domains.
- Recognize common phishing red flags.
- Understand the potential risks of phishing.
- Develop better phishing-awareness and detection skills.
- Provide safe recommendations for handling suspicious messages.

---

##  Phishing Analysis

### 1.  Fake Bank Account Alert

**Scenario:**  
A fictional message warns the recipient that their bank account will be suspended unless they verify their information immediately.

**Key Red Flags:**
- Urgent and threatening language
- Account suspension warning
- Request for sensitive information
- Suspicious verification link
- Vague sender identity

**Potential Risk:**  
Credential theft and unauthorized account access.

---

### 2.  Fake Prize / Reward Message

**Scenario:**  
A fictional message claims that the recipient has won a large reward and must verify their information before the offer expires.

**Key Red Flags:**
- Unexpected prize
- Large reward amount
- Urgent deadline
- Request for personal information
- Suspicious claim link

**Potential Risk:**  
Personal-information theft and financial fraud.

---

### 3.  Fake Social Media Security Alert

**Scenario:**  
A fictional security alert claims that a new login attempt was detected and asks the recipient to verify their account within a limited time.

**Key Red Flags:**
- Fear of unauthorized login
- Threat of account locking
- Urgent verification request
- Suspicious link
- Vague sender identity

**Potential Risk:**  
Credential theft and unauthorized account access.

---

##  Common Phishing Indicators

The following warning signs were identified during the analysis:

-  Urgent or threatening language
-  Unexpected requests or alerts
-  Suspicious or unfamiliar links
-  Requests for sensitive information
-  Vague or suspicious sender identity
-  Emotional pressure such as fear or excitement
-  Short deadlines designed to pressure the recipient

---

##  Potential Risks

Phishing attacks can potentially result in:

- Unauthorized account access
- Credential theft
- Exposure of personal information
- Financial fraud
- Compromised online accounts

---

##  How to Stay Safe

To reduce the risk of falling victim to phishing:

1. **Pause** before responding to urgent messages.
2. **Check** the sender, wording, and link carefully.
3. **Verify** requests through official websites or applications.
4. **Protect** passwords and other sensitive information.
5. **Report** suspicious messages through the appropriate channels.
6. Avoid clicking links in unexpected messages.
7. When in doubt, contact the organization through a trusted method.
