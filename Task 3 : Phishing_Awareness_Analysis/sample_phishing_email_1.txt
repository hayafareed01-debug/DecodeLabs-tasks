PHISHING AWARENESS ANALYSIS
Sample 1: Fake Bank Account Alert
========================================

ORIGINAL MESSAGE
----------------------------------------

Subject: URGENT: Your Bank Account Will Be Suspended

Dear Customer,

We detected unusual activity on your bank account.

Your account will be suspended within 24 hours unless you
verify your information immediately.

Click here to verify your account:
https://secure-bank-verification.example.com

Please provide your account details and verify your information.

Thank you,
Bank Security Team


SUSPICIOUS KEYWORDS / PHRASES
----------------------------------------

• URGENT
• unusual activity
• suspended
• within 24 hours
• verify immediately
• account details


SUSPICIOUS LINK
----------------------------------------

https://secure-bank-verification.example.com

Reason:
The domain does not clearly belong to a legitimate bank.
It is designed to appear trustworthy while requesting
account verification.


PHISHING RED FLAGS
----------------------------------------

1. Urgent language is used to pressure the recipient.
2. The message threatens account suspension.
3. The recipient is asked to verify information immediately.
4. A suspicious link is provided.
5. The sender identity is vague.
6. The message requests sensitive account information.


WHY THIS MESSAGE IS UNSAFE
----------------------------------------

This message is potentially unsafe because it uses urgency
and fear to pressure the recipient into clicking a suspicious
link and providing sensitive information. The sender is not
properly identified, and the request should be verified
through an official channel.


RECOMMENDED ACTION
----------------------------------------

Do not click the link or provide personal information.

Instead, contact the bank through its official website,
mobile application, or a trusted phone number to verify
whether the alert is genuine.
