PHISHING AWARENESS ANALYSIS
Sample 2: Fake Prize/Reward Message
========================================

ORIGINAL MESSAGE
----------------------------------------

Congratulations!

You have been selected to receive a special reward of
Rs. 50,000.

To claim your reward, verify your information immediately
using the link below:

https://reward-claim.example.com

This offer expires today. Failure to respond may result
in the loss of your reward.

Thank you,
Rewards Department


SUSPICIOUS KEYWORDS / PHRASES
----------------------------------------

• Congratulations
• selected to receive
• Rs. 50,000
• verify your information
• immediately
• expires today
• loss of your reward


SUSPICIOUS LINK
----------------------------------------

https://reward-claim.example.com

Reason:
The link does not identify a legitimate company or
organization and is associated with an unexpected reward.


PHISHING RED FLAGS
----------------------------------------

1. The recipient is promised an unexpected reward.
2. The message creates excitement with a large prize.
3. Urgent language is used.
4. The recipient is asked to provide information.
5. A suspicious link is included.
6. There is a threat of losing the reward.


WHY THIS MESSAGE IS UNSAFE
----------------------------------------

This message is potentially unsafe because it uses an
unexpected reward and time pressure to encourage the
recipient to provide information. The recipient should
verify the offer through an official source instead of
using the provided link.


RECOMMENDED ACTION
----------------------------------------

Do not click the link or provide personal information.

Verify the offer independently through the official website
or contact information of the organization that supposedly
sent the message.
