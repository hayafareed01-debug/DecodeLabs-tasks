PHISHING AWARENESS — COMPARISON TABLE
========================================

Sample 1 — Fake Bank Account Alert

Type:
Bank/Account Phishing

Main Red Flags:
• Urgent account suspension warning
• Suspicious verification link
• Request for account information
• Pressure to act within 24 hours

Potential Risk:
Credential theft and unauthorized account access.


Sample 2 — Fake Prize/Reward Message

Type:
Prize/Reward Phishing

Main Red Flags:
• Unexpected reward
• Large prize amount
• Urgent deadline
• Suspicious claim link
• Request for personal information

Potential Risk:
Personal information theft and possible financial fraud.


Sample 3 — Fake Social Media Security Alert

Type:
Account Security Phishing

Main Red Flags:
• Fake login/security alert
• Threat of account locking
• Urgent verification request
• Suspicious verification link
• Vague sender identity

Potential Risk:
Account credential theft and unauthorized account access.


COMMON PHISHING INDICATORS
========================================

1. Urgent or threatening language
2. Requests for sensitive information
3. Suspicious or unfamiliar links
4. Unexpected offers or alerts
5. Pressure to act immediately
6. Unclear or suspicious sender identity
7. Fear-based or emotionally manipulative messages


GENERAL SAFETY RECOMMENDATIONS
========================================

• Do not click suspicious links.
• Do not provide passwords or sensitive information.
• Verify messages through official channels.
• Check the sender carefully.
• Avoid acting under pressure or urgency.
• Report suspicious messages when appropriate.
• When in doubt, contact the organization directly.
